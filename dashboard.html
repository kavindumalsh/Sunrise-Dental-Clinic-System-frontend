User Guide

Follow these steps to use the Sunrise Dental Management System.

1

Overview

The first screen after signing in. Shows today's key numbers, one-click Quick Actions to jump to any task, and your five most recent appointments.

2

Register a Patient

Navigate to Patients. Fill in the patient's full name, contact number, address, and an optional email (enables email confirmations), then click Register Patient. They immediately appear in the registry and become available in the appointment form.

3

Book an Appointment

Go to Register Appointment. Select a patient, dentist, treatment type, date and time, then submit to receive a unique Appointment Number (e.g., APP-12345678). Bookings are only accepted Monday-Saturday, 08:00-17:30.

4

Appointments: Search, Bill & Cancel

Open Appointments. Search by number to view details and generate an invoice, or browse/filter the All Appointments table below and click any row to load it. Cancel Appointment marks it Cancelled (never deleted) and notifies the patient.

5

Management Reports (Administrators)

Admin accounts see a Reports menu with revenue, appointment-trend and dentist-workload charts backed by live database views, plus the ability to add new treatment types.

6

Staff Accounts (Administrators)

Admin accounts see a Staff Accounts menu to create new logins (receptionist or administrator) and remove accounts that are no longer needed. There is no public sign-up, by design — only an existing administrator can create a new account.

7

Sign Out Securely

When you're done, click Sign Out at the bottom of the sidebar. This invalidates your session on the server and returns you to the login page.
