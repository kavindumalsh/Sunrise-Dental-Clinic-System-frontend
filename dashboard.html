Help & Instructions

Step-by-step guide on using the Sunrise Dental Management System.

1. Registering an Appointment

Navigate to the "Register Appointment" tab. Fill in all patient details including the treatment type and the scheduled date/time. Click "Register Appointment" to generate a unique Appointment Number.

2. Searching & Billing

Navigate to the "Search & Billing" tab. Enter the unique Appointment Number provided during registration. Once the details are displayed, click "Generate Bill" to calculate the total cost and print the receipt.

3. Exiting the System

To safely exit the application, click the "Logout" button located at the bottom of the sidebar. This will securely log you out and return you to the login screen.
